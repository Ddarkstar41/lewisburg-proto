Four households have joined the neighborhood since spring. If you have not met them yet, they
are on Copal, Holly, and two on Northlake. The picnic in October is the easy way to say hello.

## About the oak

Visitors have picked up noticeably this summer, which is mostly a good thing. A reminder for
anyone directing friends and family here: the Seven Sisters Oak stands on private property.
It is perfectly visible from the street, and that is where visitors should stay.

The tree is roughly a thousand years old and about thirty-nine feet around. It has survived
every hurricane in recorded Louisiana history. It does not need anyone standing on its roots
for a photograph.
